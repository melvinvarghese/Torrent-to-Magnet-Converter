How it works

Choose one or more .torrent files. Your browser reads the metadata, calculates the v1 or v2 info hash, and creates a magnet link you can copy into your torrent client. Hybrid torrents include both hashes. Files are never uploaded or saved by this page; the converter does not download torrent content or connect to peers.
